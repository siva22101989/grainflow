# Chapter 9: Billing & Subscriptions

Manage your GrainFlow subscription plan, view payment history, and upgrade when your warehouse needs grow.

---

## Overview

GrainFlow uses a subscription model with four tiers. All paid plans include a 14-day free trial. You can manage your subscription from **Billing** in the sidebar.

### Yearly Billing

All plans offer a yearly option at approximately 20% off the monthly price:

- **Starter Yearly:** Rs.4,790/year (saves roughly Rs.1,198)
- **Professional Yearly:** Rs.14,390/year (saves roughly Rs.3,598)

Enterprise pricing is custom. Contact the sales team for a quote.

### What Counts as a "Storage Record"?

A storage record is one active grain deposit from a customer. For example, if 100 customers each have bags currently stored, that counts as 100 records. Completed or withdrawn records do not count toward your plan limit.

## 9.2 Viewing Your Current Plan

1. Go to **Billing** in the sidebar.
2. The **Current Plan** card shows:
   - Your plan name and status (active, trialing, etc.)
   - Next renewal date
   - Usage bar showing how many storage records you have used out of your plan limit

[Screenshot: Billing page showing current plan card with usage bar]

---

## 9.3 Upgrading Your Plan

1. Go to **Billing**.
2. Click the **Upgrade Plan** button.
3. A dialog opens showing available upgrade options with prices and feature lists.
4. Click **Upgrade to [Plan Name]**.
5. A secure Razorpay payment link opens in a new tab.
6. Complete payment using UPI, card, net banking, or any supported method.
7. Once payment is confirmed, your plan upgrades immediately.

> **Tip:** Upgrades take effect right away. You get instant access to the new plan's features and limits.

> **Important:** Downgrades apply at the end of your current billing cycle. Your existing features remain available until then.

---

## 9.4 Payment Methods

GrainFlow uses Razorpay for secure payment processing. Supported methods:

- UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc.)
- Credit and Debit Cards
- Net Banking
- Wallets

You do not need to store card details in GrainFlow. Each payment is processed through a one-time secure link.

## 9.5 Payment History

1. Go to **Billing**.
2. The **Payment History** card on the right shows your recent transactions.
3. Each entry displays:
   - Plan name
   - Payment date
   - Payment method (UPI, Card, etc.)
   - Amount (Rs.)
   - Status (success or failed)

The list shows the five most recent payments. If you have more, a note at the bottom indicates the total count.

## 9.7 Trial and Renewal

### 14-Day Free Trial

When you sign up for any paid plan, you get 14 days of full access at no cost. No credit card is required to start the trial.

After 14 days:
- If you add a payment method and pay, your subscription continues.
- If you do not pay, your account reverts to the Free tier. Your data is preserved, but features above the Free limit become unavailable.

### Renewal

Paid subscriptions renew automatically. Before renewal:
- You will see your next renewal date on the Billing page.
- If payment fails, you will be notified and given a grace period to update payment.

### 30-Day Refund Policy

If you are not satisfied with a paid plan, contact support within 30 days of payment for a full refund.

## Tips

- **Start with Free to learn the system.** You can always upgrade later without losing any data.
- **Choose Yearly billing if you plan to use GrainFlow long-term.** The 20% discount adds up over a year.
- **Watch the usage bar.** If you are approaching your record limit, plan your upgrade before you hit the cap -- you will not be able to add new records once the limit is reached.
