## Quick Start Checklist

New to GrainFlow? Follow these steps:

1. **Sign up** at the login page with your name, email/phone, and password
2. **Create your warehouse** with name, location, and capacity
3. **Add warehouse lots** (storage zones) under Settings > Lots
4. **Add your first customer** under Customers > Add Customer
5. **Record your first inflow** under Inflow with commodity, bags, and lot
6. **Invite your team** under Settings > Team

You're ready to go. See [Chapter 1](01-getting-started.md) for the full walkthrough.
