# Chapter 1: Getting Started

This chapter covers how to create your GrainFlow account, log in, set up your warehouse, and find your way around the dashboard.

---

## 1.1 Creating Your Account

To start using GrainFlow, you need to create an account.

1. Open GrainFlow in your browser.
2. On the login page, click **Sign up**.
3. Fill in the following details:
   - **Full Name** -- your name as it should appear in the system.
   - **Email** -- your email address (e.g., name@company.com).
   - **Password** -- choose a password with at least 6 characters. The strength indicator will show Weak, Fair, or Strong as you type.
   - **Mobile Number** -- your 10-digit mobile number.
4. Click **Sign Up**.
5. You will be redirected to the dashboard to set up your warehouse.

[Screenshot: Sign up page with fields filled in]

> **Tip:** Use a strong password (8+ characters) for better security. The strength bar turns green when your password is strong enough.

---

## 1.2 Logging In

Once your account exists, log in as follows:

1. Open the GrainFlow login page.
2. Enter your **Email or Mobile Number** in the first field.
   - You can type your 10-digit mobile number directly -- no need to add +91.
   - Or use your full email address.
3. Enter your **Password**.
4. Click **Login**.

[Screenshot: Login page]

If you have forgotten your password, click the **Forgot password?** link below the password field and follow the instructions sent to your email.

> **Tip:** Logging in with your mobile number is the fastest option. Just type your 10 digits and your password.

---

## 1.3 Setting Up Your First Warehouse

After signing up for the first time, GrainFlow will ask you to create your warehouse. This is a one-time setup.

1. Enter **Warehouse Name** (e.g., "Main Godown" or "Sai Storage").
2. Enter **Location** (e.g., "Guntur, Andhra Pradesh").
3. Enter your **Phone** number and **Email** (optional).
4. Enter **Total Capacity (Bags)** -- this is the maximum number of bags your warehouse can hold.
5. Click **Get Started**.

[Screenshot: Create Warehouse onboarding screen]

Your warehouse is now ready. You will see the main dashboard.

> **Important:** The capacity you set here is used to show how full your warehouse is on the dashboard. You can update it later in Settings.

---

## 1.4 Staff and Team Members

If you are a staff member joining an existing warehouse (not the owner), you will see a **Join Warehouse** screen instead of the setup screen. Enter the warehouse code provided by your owner or admin to join.

---

## 1.5 The Dashboard

The dashboard is the first screen you see after logging in. It gives you a quick overview of your warehouse operations.

[Screenshot: Dashboard with hero section and stats]

The dashboard is organized into these sections:

### Hero Section
At the top, you will see:
- **Warehouse Name** -- the name of your current warehouse.
- **Total Stock** -- how many bags are currently stored.
- **Capacity** -- a visual bar showing how full your warehouse is.
- **Active Records** -- the number of storage records that are currently open (stock not yet withdrawn).

### Quick Shortcuts
Below the hero section, shortcut buttons let you jump directly to common tasks like creating a new inflow, processing an outflow, or adding a customer.

### Overview Stats
Key numbers displayed as cards:
- Total stock in the warehouse.
- Number of active storage records.
- Financial summaries (total billed, total collected, pending amounts).

### Market Prices
A widget showing current market prices for common agricultural commodities, updated regularly.

### Recent Activity
A timeline of the most recent inflows and outflows, showing date, customer name, commodity, and bags.

## 1.6 Navigation

GrainFlow has a sidebar menu (on desktop) or a bottom navigation bar (on mobile) with the following sections:

| Menu Item      | What It Does                                      |
|----------------|---------------------------------------------------|
| Dashboard      | Overview of your warehouse                        |
| Customers      | Manage customer profiles and balances              |
| Inflow         | Record new stock arrivals                          |
| Outflow        | Process stock withdrawals and generate bills       |
| Payments       | View pending payments and record collections       |
| Expenses       | Track warehouse operational costs                  |
| Settings       | Manage lots, crops, team members, and preferences  |

To navigate, click the menu item on the sidebar or tap it on mobile.

> **Tip:** The breadcrumb trail at the top of every page shows where you are. For example: Dashboard > Customers > Add Customer. Click any breadcrumb to go back to that level.

---

## 1.7 Dark Mode

GrainFlow supports dark mode for comfortable use in low-light conditions.

To switch between light and dark mode:

1. Go to **Settings** or look for the theme toggle in the top navigation bar.
2. Choose **Light**, **Dark**, or **System** (matches your device setting).

> **Tip:** If you work in a godown with bright sunlight during the day and dim light in the evening, use **System** mode so GrainFlow switches automatically.

---

## 1.8 Subscription Alerts

If your subscription is nearing expiry or has expired, a banner will appear at the top of the dashboard alerting you. Contact your administrator or follow the renewal link in the alert to continue using GrainFlow without interruption.
